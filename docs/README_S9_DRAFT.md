# README draft: Season 9 entry (for the Phase 5 museum)

### Season 9 · Tides 🌊

A weekly season. Every Friday at 21:00 UK time the tide turns: wins and upgrades reset, the top three take a medal, and a new community goal begins. Medals, cosmetics and the fish Encyclopaedia carry over, so there's a short race every week and a long chase across the season. Auto-spin is free and keeps playing while you're away, and new players meet the panels one at a time.

Retired: Prestige, Loadouts, the Singularity and the Aquarium.
