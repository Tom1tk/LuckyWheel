## 🏛 The Season Museum

The wheel has been reset nine times. Each season tried something new, broke something old, and left the game a little stranger than before.

### Season 1 · The Prototype
*19–21 March 2026*

A wheel, a fish, and a shop. Within two days it had seasons, a roulette-style red and green makeover, and **infinite upgrades**. Wins were stored as unlimited-precision numbers from the start, which turned out to matter. The winner finished on a **136-digit number**.

**What came next:** the numbers were already absurd, and every season after this one tried to tame them.

### Season 2
*21–27 March*

The first anti-cheat season: per-account rate limits, a fish-click cap and a click budget, plus an auto-guard against losing streaks. It was also the first season with a page theme and a season history. The first real rollover bug arrived here too: upgrades survived the reset until a fix landed on the last day. The winning total had **348 digits**, the biggest number the wheel has ever seen.

### Season 3
*27 March – 4 April*

Wins and losses became **two currencies**, and the shop started charging in both. A **community pot** gave everyone a shared goal for the first time. The winner settled for a modest **59 digits**.

### Season 4
*4–10 April*

An economy rebalance, five new fish, and a leaderboard that finally hid players with zero wins. A balance hotfix followed the next day. The winner: **73 digits**.

### Season 5
*10–17 April*

The season the numbers came back to Earth. A **streak soft cap**, **dice charges** and **tier gating** brought the winning total down to about **116 billion**, a mere 12 digits. The Ocean Casino background debuted, and each season's page theme was now granted automatically at rollover.

### Season 6 · Night Ocean
*17–24 April*

The fish got a job. **Cast & Reel fishing** arrived with species to collect, fishing gear to buy, a night-ocean theme and an in-game **chat**. Anti-cheat landed on launch day. When the season ended, the game went into its first **hiatus**: a waiting-room screen with a mini wheel, last season's winners and a countdown. Winner: about **269 billion**.

### Season 7 · Endless
*24 April – 9 May*

Designed to never end. Auto-spin moved **server-side** and kept playing while you were away, upgrade paths went infinite, and the **class system** and **fish exchange** arrived. Then, at midnight, the season reset itself anyway. A forgotten end date fired the automatic rollover, and everyone's upgrades were wiped.

The fix shipped the next morning: auto-rollover was removed, the true Season 7 winners were restored (about **83 billion**), and an **apology popup** explained what went wrong and who was responsible.

### Season 7.7
*9 May – 26 June*

Season 7, continued after the accidental reset. Its big mid-season update, **High Stakes** (21 June), brought the **wager system**, **wheel modes**, daily **bounties**, **community goals** and loadouts. Winner: about **298 billion**.

### Season 8 · Casino
*26 June – 3 July*

The whole page became a casino floor: **pick-your-risk wagers**, all-or-nothing **double-downs**, **insurance tokens**, **prestige** and a **Long Shot** wheel mode. Launch night was a scramble, with a 13-minute half-deployed window and a rollover fired by hand. When the season ran out on 3 July, nobody turned the wheel again, and the game went quiet for three months. A first attempt at Season 9, "Arcade", was built and shelved.

### Season 9 · Tides
*October 2026 –*

The revival. Instead of one long season that a single player can run away with, Season 9 runs in **weekly tides**. Every Friday at 21:00 UK time, wins reset and the top three take a **medal**. Medals, cosmetics and your fish Encyclopaedia carry over, and auto-spin is free for everyone. Prestige, loadouts, the Singularity and the Aquarium were retired to keep the weekly race fair.
